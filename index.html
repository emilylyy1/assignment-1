ASSIGNMENT 3 · FINAL PROJECT

Little Keys — A Musical Playground

Little Keys is a browser-based musical toy designed for toddlers to explore sound through large, colourful piano keys. Each press creates a note and a playful visual response, encouraging curiosity and experimentation without requiring musical knowledge or reading skills.

Design scenario: A musical toy designed for toddlers to explore music in a fun and engaging way. The prompt is to design a way for toddlers to play music and feel excited about their creations.

Response to the scenario: Little Keys uses a simple keyboard, large touch targets, distinct notes and immediate visual and audio feedback. It avoids complex controls so children can explore through cause and effect.
